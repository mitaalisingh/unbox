Privacy notice

Last updated 26 October 2026.

Unbox is a student project. I wrote this in plain words. A lawyer has not checked it.

The short version

  • Your photos are private. Only you can see them, unless you choose to share.
  • We remove the hidden location from every photo you upload.
  • The AI that names your photos runs on our own server. Your photos are not sent to any AI company.
  • No ads. No tracking. We never sell your data.
  • You can delete a photo, a trip, or your whole account whenever you want.

What we keep about you

  • Your email. So you can log in and get messages from us.
  • Your password. We can't read it. It is stored in a locked-up form.
  • Your first name, if you gave one. We use it to say hello.
  • The time you agreed to this notice, and that you said you are 18 or older.

We do not ask for your phone number, your address or any payment details.

What you add

Your trips, boxes, item names, weights, notes and photos. Also a trip picture, if you upload one.

Small things saved on your phone

A tiny note called a cookie keeps you logged in. Your phone also remembers choices like Grid or Swipe, and whether you have seen the instructions. These are not used to follow you around.

Simple records we keep

The website's host writes basic logs, like the time and which page was opened. This helps keep the service safe. If someone guesses the password of a shared trip too many times, we note it so we can slow them down.

Your photos and the AI

When you upload a photo, we do three things:

  1. Remove the hidden location and camera details.
  2. Make the photo smaller.
  3. Throw away the big original.

Then a small AI program on our own server looks at the photo and suggests a name. It is open source. It is not another company's AI service. Your photos are kept with the storage company listed below.

Which companies help us

  • Supabase keeps the database, the logins and your photo files.
  • Render runs the app.
  • GitHub keeps only the app's code. It never gets your data.

Who can see what you add

  • You. Other users cannot see your trips.
  • People you share with. With Share by QR, anyone who has the QR or link can look at that trip. They can't change anything. If you set a password, they need it. If you don't, anyone who gets the QR can look, so use a password for private things.
  • People you give a trip code to. They get their own copy, photos too. After that the copy is theirs, and we can't take it back.
  • Me, the maker. The tools I use can show me stored data. I won't look unless you ask me for help, or I need to fix a safety problem.

Deleting your data

Delete a trip and its items and photos are removed. Delete your account (Profile, then Delete my account) and your login, trips, items and photos are removed. A copy that a friend already made with a trip code stays with them. Deleted data may stay for a short time in the backups of the companies above.

Your choices, and how to reach me

You can see, change and delete most things yourself in the app. For anything else, {% if contact_email %}email me at {{ contact_email }}{% else %}write on the project's page at github.com/mitaalisingh/unbox. That page is public{% endif %}. Unbox is only for people aged 18 or older.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes too.
