Privacy notice

Last updated 26 October 2026.

Unbox is a student project. This notice is a plain-language draft and has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

Who we are

Unbox is a college project by Mitaali Singh. To ask a question or make a request, open an issue at github.com/mitaalisingh/unbox.

What we collect, and why

  • Your account: your email address (to log you in, confirm your account and reset your password), your password (we never see it; our login provider keeps it scrambled), an optional first name (to greet you), and a record that you confirmed you are 18 or older and accepted this notice, with the time.
  • What you add: trips, boxes, item names, weights, tags and notes, your photos, and a picture for a trip if you upload one.
  • Small settings kept on your phone or browser: a login cookie so you stay logged in, and choices such as Grid or Swipe, or that you have seen the instructions. These are not used to track you.
  • Basic technical records: our host keeps standard request logs (for example the time, the page and your internet address) to keep the service running and safe. Wrong password attempts on a shared trip are counted using a scrambled version of your internet address, so guessing can be slowed down.

We do not ask for your phone number, home address or payment details.

Photos and the AI

Photos are kept in private storage and shown through links that expire after an hour. When you upload a photo, we remove location data and other hidden information from it, shrink it, and do not keep the original.

To suggest names, our own server looks at your photos with an open-source model. Your photos are not sent to any AI company or other third party for this. They do pass through the servers of our hosting providers, listed below.

Who handles your data

  • Supabase stores the database, the login system and your files.
  • Render runs the app.
  • GitHub holds only the program code, never your data.

These providers act for us. Their servers may be outside India.

Who can see your data

  • You. Other accounts cannot see your trips.
  • Anyone you share with. If you turn on Share by QR, anyone who has a QR label or link for that trip can look at it, read-only, without an account. If you set a password, they need it. With no password, the link itself is the only protection, so use a password for anything private.
  • Whoever you give a trip code to. They get their own copy of the trip, photos included. After that, the copy is theirs and we cannot take it back.
  • The developer. Because of how the service is built, the developer can technically see stored data in the provider dashboards. I will not look unless you ask for support or it is needed to keep the service safe.

Deleting your data

You can delete a trip, which removes its items and photos, or delete your whole account under Profile → Delete my account, which removes your login, trips, items and photos. Copies that someone already made with a trip code stay with them. Deleted data may stay briefly in our providers' backups.

Your choices

You can see, change and delete most of your data yourself in the app. For anything else, use the contact above. Unbox is for people aged 18 and over.

No selling, no ads

We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use advertising or analytics trackers.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes too.
