Privacy notice

Draft for a student project. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

What we store

Your email address, a scrambled password (handled by our login provider), and what you add: trips, boxes, item names, weights and photos. We don't ask for your phone number or address.

Photos

Photos are kept in private storage and shown through short-lived links. Location data inside photos is removed when you upload them. We don't keep full-size originals.

Who can see your data

You can. Anyone you share a trip's viewer password with can view that trip. The developer can technically see stored data in the database dashboard, and will not look unless you ask for support.

AI

By default, photos are named on your own device and not sent anywhere for that step. Any option that sends photos to another company will be off until you switch it on, with a clear warning.

Deleting

You can archive a trip (hidden but kept) or delete it for good, which removes its data. You can also ask us to delete your whole account.

No selling, no tracking

We don't sell or share your data and we don't use advertising trackers.

Who we are

Unbox is a college project by Mitaali Singh. This notice will be updated, with the date, as the project grows.
