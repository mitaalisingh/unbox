A few things about the person who made this

Who am I

I'm Mitaali Singh. I built Unbox as a college project, on my own, from the first sketch to the live site: the design, the app, the photo-naming AI and the privacy rules.

Why Unbox

It began with a real move. I packed my brother's things to send across India, and I wanted him to scan a box and see what was inside before he opened anything. He loved it, so I turned it into something anyone can use.

What it's made of

PythonFlaskSupabasePostgres + pgvectorONNX RuntimeSigLIP2Renderpytest

What I'm proud of

  • The AI that names your photos runs on a free server, with no paid AI service. I shrank the model from about 1.4 GB to about 350 MB to make it fit.
  • On my test photos it got the name right first time about 8 times in 10, and you fix the rest with one tap.
  • Your photos stay private: location data is stripped, files are kept in a private store, and you can delete your account and everything in it.
  • Over 160 automated tests, so changes don't quietly break things.
